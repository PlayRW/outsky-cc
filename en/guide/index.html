OutSky VPN/Setup guide

How to connect OutSky VPN

Get a subscription link from the bot and add it to an app — it only takes a couple of minutes.

Step 1

Get your subscription link

  1. Open @OutSkyVPN_bot and tap “Start”. If the bot asks for a language, pick one.
  2. Subscribe to the @outskycc channel and tap the ✅ button to confirm.
  3. Tap “🎁 Free trial”, then tap 🎁 again to activate it.
  4. The bot sends your subscription link. Tap it to copy. You can always find the link under “📱 My subscription”.

iPhone & iPad

Free INCY app · iOS or iPadOS 15 or later

1Install INCY

Get it from the App Store: incy. It’s available in the Russian App Store too.

The App Store has look-alikes with similar names, such as “Incy VPN Випиэн” and “INCY VPN ВПН Випиэн”. The real app is called simply “incy”, and the developer is LLC ITDEV.

2Add your subscription

  1. In the bot, tap your subscription link to copy it.
  2. Open INCY and tap “Paste” on the home screen (or “+ Add” and paste the link). If your iPhone asks for permission to paste, allow it.
  3. You’ll see a list of servers by country.

3Connect

  1. Choose a server. The main option is the one named after the country; “HS2” and “XHTTP” are backups in case the main one doesn’t connect. On the free trial each country has one connection option: if a server won’t connect, pick another country. The “🔒 Ещё 7 стран — в подписке” line is informational and does not connect.
  2. Tap the big connect button.
  3. The first time you connect, your iPhone will ask to add a VPN configuration — tap “Allow” and confirm with your passcode or Face ID.

Done — a VPN icon appears in the status bar. The app keeps your subscription up to date on its own.

Windows

Free INCY app · 64-bit Windows 10 or 11

The desktop version of INCY is still early, so expect some bugs. If you only need VPN in the browser, the OutSky VPN extension for Chrome is more reliable.

1Install INCY

  1. Download the INCY installer for Windows from INCY’s official GitHub page. It works on regular PCs and on laptops with ARM processors.
  2. Run the installer and follow the prompts. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click “More info” → “Run anyway”.

2Add your subscription

  1. In the bot, tap your subscription link to copy it.
  2. Open INCY and click “Paste” (or “+ Add” and paste the link).
  3. You’ll see a list of servers by country.

3Connect

  1. Choose “System proxy” mode — it’s enough for browsers and most programs.
  2. Choose a server without “HS2” in its name: those servers don’t work in the Windows version yet. On the free trial each country has one connection option: if a country only has an “HS2” server, or it won’t connect, pick another country. The “🔒 Ещё 7 стран — в подписке” line is informational and does not connect.
  3. Click the connect button.

To see which processor you have, open the Apple menu (the Apple logo in the top-left corner) → “About This Mac”.

  • Intel — download INCY for Intel Macs from INCY’s official GitHub page, open the .dmg file and drag INCY into the Applications folder. This version is still early, so expect some bugs.

Google Chrome

OutSky VPN extension · VPN in the browser only; other programs work as usual

1Install the extension

  1. Open OutSky VPN in the Chrome Web Store, click “Add to Chrome” and confirm.
  2. Pin the icon: click the puzzle-piece icon to the right of the address bar, then the pin next to OutSky VPN.

2Add your subscription

  1. In the bot, tap your subscription link to copy it.
  2. Click the OutSky VPN icon, paste the link into the “Paste subscription link” field and click “Activate”.

3Connect

  1. Click the big button — the status changes to “Protected”.
  2. To switch location, click “Change”.

Good to know

  • Routing is in the extension settings: “Smart” — Russian sites open directly, everything else goes through VPN; “Full tunnel” — all sites go through VPN.
  • “Auto-connect” in settings turns VPN on when Chrome starts.
  • Need VPN for the whole computer? Install INCY: see the guides for Windows and Mac.
